## Why more gain does not solve these failures

Reconstruct the two-hop activation from the frozen phase-1 seeds and snapshot's distinct directed traversable neighbours, including full sender degree. Keep the two originally selected damping values. Reconstruct the ordinary warm assembly score as `intercept + gain * slope`: direct candidates have their frozen daemon/direct intercept and zero slope; graph-only candidates have a daemon intercept and normalized propagation slope. The reconstruction exactly matches all **12 selected-arm top-10 challenge lists**, with maximum absolute score error `7.1e-15`. No Canonical nodes or forced warnings complicate these cases. For each missing support, compare its score line with every candidate's line and check all nonnegative gain intervals separated by their crossings. This is an exploratory mathematical diagnosis of historical frames, not a new renderer experiment or a parameter selection.

Each support has **at least 12 competitors** whose intercept and slope are both no smaller and at least one is strictly greater. Those competitors stay ahead for every positive gain; the zero-gain order also excludes the supports. Thus no gain alone can place any of these supports in ordinary top-10 at the fixed selected damping values. The original failure occurs before token fitting. This goes beyond the first pilot's score-scale diagnosis, but says nothing about another damping value, candidate generator, direction, fusion rule or propagation method.

## Evaluation decision

The replay and diagnosis supply no adoption case. Keep #175 and the draft PR open, with propagation test-only and production settings unchanged. Further gain escalation at these selected damping values has no ranking headroom for the held-out supports. Any subsequent effectiveness test needs fresh, independently reviewed questions with atomic facets, two-hop-only and relevant-hub support, complete candidate judgements and quality/latency measurements. Previously inspected questions can remain regression cases, not fresh confirmation.
