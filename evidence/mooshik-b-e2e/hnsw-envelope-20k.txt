Caveat: a uniformly random high-dimensional corpus is the worst case for hnsw (all pairwise
cosines cluster near 0, so ranks beyond the self-match are near-ties). A real embedding corpus
is clustered and will do better. The point is not this number: it is that H3 ships an envelope
measured at 9 and 22 vectors, where hnsw cannot diverge at all, and no measurement exists at
any corpus size where the approximation is real.
