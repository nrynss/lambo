The Linux RTX 4070 SUPER rig tested a direct upgrade from v0.12.0 to v0.15.0 on
an 11,270-concept SQLite session. All 330 replay outcomes and the ordered top eight on
all 200 paired recall queries matched. No writes failed across 16 load runs.

## CUDA results

The recall time in this table comes from the server logs. The offline rows report
wall-clock means.

| Measurement | v0.12.0 | v0.15.0 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sequential recall p50 / p99 | 11.05 / 49.72 ms | 11.09 / 47.35 ms |
| Session load mean | 826.4 ms | 832.9 ms |
| Canonization dry run mean | 27.15 s | 26.97 s |

Across four paired 16-agent tests, the ratio of v0.15.0 recall p99 to v0.12.0 ranged from
0.97 to 1.03. The sequential derive acknowledgement sample had a different split between
fast and slow calls. The experiment left the cause unresolved.

Swarm dry-run reports matched. Under Solo, 498 concepts reached Candidate on v0.12.0
and 496 on v0.15.0.
