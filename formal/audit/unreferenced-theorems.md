# Unreferenced theorems in the Lean models

Generated by `python3 -I scripts/formal/audit-theorems.py --out formal/audit/unreferenced-theorems.md` from the models as built.

7 theorems this audit found unreferenced were deleted in the change that added it; `formal/audit/deleted-theorems.tsv` lists them with their reasons (and `check-decl-equivalence.py --deletions` reads it), and so does the section "Deleted" at the end.

A theorem counts as referenced when another declaration's proof or definition uses it (read from Lean's environment, through the constants Lean generates), an `example` names it, a docs page's `lean:` anchor cites it, a claims ledger under `formal/claims/` names it with its model file, a comment in `src/` or `tests/` names it (as `Model.name`, or in backticks near a mention of its model file), or `Audit.lean` prints its axioms. The rest are listed below, each with a class:

* **(a)** a standalone property worth keeping, ideally cited from a test, doc or ledger. Criteria: its file's header, `formal/README.md` or the CHANGELOG names it as a result; its doc comment opens with a bold title (the models' mark for a result), quotes the Rust or names a Rust test; or it is a closed statement about a concrete case.
* **(b)** a deletion candidate, only when shown to be one. Criteria: a referenced theorem that stays states the same thing, or supersedes it (its name with a current-model suffix such as `fooW`, or the current counterpart its doc comment names) and it is not stated as a result (a bold-titled doc comment, such as a finding's, keeps it in (a)); or it is a dead helper, an undocumented `rfl` unfolding of a definition that nothing uses or names.
* **(c)** unclear: anything else, such as an undocumented lemma that may be a property in its own right (an asymmetry, an invariant) or a leftover, or a `@[simp]` lemma `simp` may use unnamed.

Precedence: a same-statement twin (b); a result named in a header, the README or the CHANGELOG, or a bold title (a); a superseding counterpart (b); the other (a) criteria; a `@[simp]` lemma (c); a dead helper (b); the rest (c). The result is a first sort for the owner to confirm. `Drain/History.lean` describes no current Rust and is kept as the evidence for its findings. Names are given below their model's namespace (`Cmd.mem_all` is `Lambo.Resolve.Cmd.mem_all`).
