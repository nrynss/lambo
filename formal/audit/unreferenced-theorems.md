# Unreferenced theorems in the Lean models

Generated by `python3 -I scripts/formal/audit-theorems.py --out formal/audit/unreferenced-theorems.md` from the models as built.

70 theorems this audit found unreferenced were deleted in the change that added it; `formal/audit/deleted-theorems.tsv` lists them with their reasons (and `check-decl-equivalence.py --deletions` reads it), and so does the section "Deleted" at the end.

A theorem counts as referenced when another declaration's proof or definition uses it (read from Lean's environment, through the constants Lean generates), an `example` names it, a docs page's `lean:` anchor cites it, a claims ledger under `formal/claims/` names it with its model file, a comment in `src/` or `tests/` names it (as `Model.name`, or in backticks near a mention of its model file), or `Audit.lean` prints its axioms. The rest are listed below, each with a class:

* **(a)** documented and reviewed against the rule (formal/README.md, "How the models are written"): a person read its statement, its doc comment and the Rust it names, and the doc comment says truthfully, no more broadly than the statement, what it guarantees about which Rust behaviour. The verdict (`keep`, or `narrowed` when the doc comment was narrowed to the statement) and its reason are in `formal/audit/reviewed-theorems.tsv`.
* **(b)** a deletion candidate, only when shown to be one. Criteria: a referenced theorem that stays states the same thing, or supersedes it (its name with a current-model suffix such as `fooW`, or the current counterpart its doc comment names) and it is not stated as a result (a bold-titled doc comment, such as a finding's, keeps it out of (b)); or it is a dead helper, an undocumented `rfl` unfolding of a definition that nothing uses or names.
* **(c)** not settled: an undocumented lemma or a documented theorem not yet reviewed, which `--check` fails on, or a `@[simp]` lemma `simp` may use without naming it.

Precedence: a same-statement twin (b); a review verdict (a); a superseding counterpart (b); a `@[simp]` lemma (c); a dead helper (b); the rest (c). `Drain/History.lean` describes no current Rust and is kept as the evidence for its findings. Names are given below their model's namespace (`Reach.trans` is `Lambo.Cite.Reach.trans`).
